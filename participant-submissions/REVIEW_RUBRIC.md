# Participant Solution Review Rubric

| Assessment Area | Weight | Review Standard |
|---|---:|---|
| Challenge interpretation | 15% | Scope and business question are understood correctly |
| Data integrity | 20% | Raw data is preserved; transformations and exclusions are documented |
| Reproducibility | 20% | Another reviewer can reproduce the result from repository files |
| HRBP and business relevance | 15% | Recommendations connect people, operations and management decisions |
| Decision usefulness | 10% | Outputs help a defined user make a clear decision |
| Documentation | 10% | README, assumptions, limitations and screenshots are complete |
| Ethics and governance | 10% | Synthetic status, privacy and human-review boundaries are maintained |

## Review Gates

A submission cannot be accepted when it:

- contains real personal or confidential data;
- exposes credentials or private URLs;
- modifies canonical raw data without an approved correction process;
- automates hiring, termination, promotion or discipline;
- presents synthetic results as real organisational performance;
- cannot be reproduced;
- contains unreviewed executable or macro content.

## Outcome Definitions

| Outcome | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Accepted | Meets the required standard for `participant-review` |
| Accepted with limitations | Useful with clearly documented constraints |
| Revision required | Correctable gaps remain |
| Showcase candidate | Demonstrates exceptional clarity, originality or reusability |
| Declined | Material quality, ethics, security, licensing or originality issue |

Promotion from `participant-review` to `main` requires a separate maintainer-controlled pull request and all required status checks.
