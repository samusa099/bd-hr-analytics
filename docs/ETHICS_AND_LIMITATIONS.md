## 🎯 Purpose

This document defines the ethical boundaries for the **Bangladesh SME HR Strategy Transformation** portfolio project.

The project is designed for:

- HR analytics learning;
- dashboard and reporting practice;
- portfolio demonstration;
- interview discussion;
- data-model and KPI experimentation.

It is **not** a production HR decision system.

> Every employee, company, salary, performance, absence, recruitment and risk record in this repository is synthetic.

---

## ✅ Acceptable Uses

You may use the project to:

- practise Excel, Power BI, Looker Studio, Python and SQL;
- demonstrate responsible people-analytics workflows;
- compare departments and trends at an aggregate level;
- create mock leadership reports;
- test KPI definitions and data models;
- discuss how HR interventions could be evaluated;
- teach why human judgement remains necessary.

---

## ⛔ Prohibited Uses

Do not use this project, its risk logic or its outputs to:

- terminate, discipline, promote or reject a real employee;
- rank real employees without documented governance;
- infer protected or sensitive characteristics;
- monitor individuals covertly;
- replace employee voice, investigation or due process;
- claim that a before-and-after result proves causation;
- present synthetic results as a real company achievement;
- deploy an AI or predictive model without validation and human oversight.

---

## 🧭 Responsible Decision Framework

Before using any employee-level output, ask:

1. **Purpose:** Is the business question legitimate and clearly documented?
2. **Minimum data:** Are only the necessary fields being used?
3. **Quality:** Are definitions, missing values and time periods validated?
4. **Fairness:** Could the metric disadvantage a group or create a proxy for a sensitive attribute?
5. **Context:** Has the employee or manager context been considered?
6. **Human review:** Is a qualified person reviewing the result before action?
7. **Appeal:** Is there a way to question or correct the information?
8. **Retention:** Is the data stored only as long as required?

---

## 🚦Using the People-Risk Score

The synthetic people-risk score is a **discussion prompt**, not a statement of truth about an employee.

### Appropriate interpretation

- identify where a supportive conversation may be useful;
- review workload, overtime, engagement and development needs;
- prioritize aggregate workforce investigation;
- test dashboard filtering and conditional formatting.

### Inappropriate interpretation

- “high risk” means the employee will resign;
- the employee should be denied promotion;
- the employee should receive disciplinary action;
- the score is accurate without human validation;
- the score can be transferred to a real company unchanged.

## 📉 Analytical Limitations

| Limitation | What it means | Required response |
|---|---|---|
| Synthetic data | Patterns are designed for learning | Do not claim real-world findings |
| Descriptive before/after analysis | Movement may have many causes | Avoid causal claims |
| Simplified risk logic | Important personal and operational context is absent | Require human review |
| Limited variables | The model cannot represent the full employee experience | Combine data with qualitative evidence |
| No external benchmark validation | Targets are illustrative | Validate benchmarks before real use |
| Potential metric bias | Some measures may reward visibility rather than contribution | Review definitions and outcomes |
| Small simulated periods | Trends may be unstable | Use longer periods in real analysis |

---

## 🤖 Responsible AI Requirements

Any AI-assisted HR use case should include:

- a clearly defined task and accountable owner;
- approved data access;
- human review before output is used;
- logging of material errors;
- bias and performance checks;
- employee-facing transparency where relevant;
- a manual alternative when AI is unsuitable;
- periodic review and a stop condition.

AI may assist with drafting, summarising and anomaly detection, but it should not make final employment decisions.

## 🧑‍💻 Usability by Audience

### Portfolio reviewers

Use this document to understand that the project demonstrates not only analytics capability, but also governance and professional judgement.

### HR professionals

Use the safeguards as a template before introducing employee-risk scoring, AI-assisted reporting or predictive people analytics.

### Students and beginners

Read this file before interpreting the employee-risk dataset. Practise explaining why a useful correlation is not automatically a fair employment decision.

### Interview presentation

A strong statement is:

> The dashboard supports prioritisation, but employee-level outputs require context, human review and due process. I would never use a synthetic risk score as an automatic employment decision.

## ✅ Pre-Release Ethics Gate

- [ ] The repository clearly states that all data is synthetic.
- [ ] No real name, email, phone number, address or confidential identifier is present.
- [ ] Dashboard pages display a synthetic-data label.
- [ ] Risk bands are presented as review prompts only.
- [ ] No documentation claims proven causation.
- [ ] High-risk findings use labels or icons, not colour alone.
- [ ] The release notes include an ethics and limitations statement.
- [ ] The final ZIP contains this document.

---

## 📬 Reporting a Concern

Open a GitHub issue describing:

- the affected file or dashboard;
- the ethical, privacy or fairness concern;
- the potential impact;
- a recommended correction, when available.

Do not include real employee data in an issue.
