## 📖 Portfolio Story

The fictional company **Nabodoy Commerce & Services Ltd.** is moving from startup operations toward an SME operating model.

Headcount growth has created connected risks:

- high turnover and early attrition;
- absenteeism and overtime pressure;
- slow recruitment;
- inconsistent performance management;
- manager capability gaps;
- weak compliance visibility;
- decisions based on fragmented information.

The portfolio demonstrates how Musa, acting as an HR Executive, would move from processing HR activity to building a strategic people-management response.

## ⭐ STAR Presentation Examples

### 1. Workforce-risk diagnosis

**Situation:** A growing startup was experiencing turnover, absence and overtime pressure.

**Task:** Identify which departments required leadership attention and explain the connected drivers.

**Action:** Standardised KPI definitions, combined monthly and department data, and created a people-risk scorecard.

**Result:** Produced a prioritised management view showing where retention, workload and manager action plans were required.

### 2. Recruitment improvement

**Situation:** Recruitment focused on filling vacancies but did not track source quality or early retention.

**Task:** Create a more business-focused talent-acquisition view.

**Action:** Built measures for time-to-fill, conversion, source quality, cost and 90-day retention.

**Result:** Demonstrated how HR could improve hiring speed while monitoring post-hire quality.

### 3. Manager capability and change

**Situation:** New managers had technical knowledge but inconsistent people-management capability.

**Task:** Build a measurable development and adoption approach.

**Action:** Designed manager learning paths, pre/post assessment, behaviour application measures and adoption tracking.

**Result:** Connected training activity with manager effectiveness and team performance indicators.

## 🧑‍💻 How to Use This Portfolio

### In a CV

Use a compact project entry:

**Bangladesh SME HR Strategy Transformation — People Analytics Portfolio**  
Built a synthetic HR transformation case using Excel, Power BI, Looker Studio, Python and SQL; analysed turnover, recruitment, manager capability, employee experience and compliance; and designed an executive intervention scorecard.

### On LinkedIn

Share:

- one executive dashboard image;
- the business problem;
- three key insights;
- the tools used;
- the synthetic-data disclaimer;
- the repository link.

### In an interview

Do not spend the full discussion describing charts. Focus on:

1. the business problem;
2. how the metrics connect;
3. why the interventions were selected;
4. how ownership and adoption would be managed;
5. what the data cannot prove;
6. what decision leadership should make next.

### In a promotion discussion

Use the project as supporting evidence of readiness, not as a substitute for real workplace results.

Connect each portfolio capability to actual examples from your work, such as recruitment coordination, employee documentation, reporting, intern supervision, performance tracking or stakeholder communication.
